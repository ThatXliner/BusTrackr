## Exact generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: alpha-cutout foliage texture for foliage cards in a real-time 3D Bay Area campus scene. Generate one original photographic-looking irregular spray of broadleaf evergreen foliage, with about 25–35 small leathery oval leaves on fine branching woody twigs. Full branch cluster isolated on a genuinely transparent RGBA background, including transparent gaps between leaves. Roughly circular loose silhouette, evenly occupying 85% of a square image, all leaf edges inside the frame. Leaves vary naturally in size, angle, and olive/dark green hue, some undersides and slight curling. Fine veins and realistic surface roughness, crisp cutout edges, no halo. Flat diffuse neutral daylight/albedo lighting with subtle local occlusion only, no cast shadow on any ground or backdrop. This is one foliage spray seen frontally for a game texture, not a whole tree, not a fern, not conifer needles, not a symmetrical botanical diagram. No labels, no text, no border, no watermark. 1024 by 1024 square requested.

The bake clears transparent RGB to a muted foliage green to reduce black edge contamination during filtering. This is an approximate edge treatment, not true alpha dilation. Leaf-edge color and the LOD switch still require closer scrutiny. Approximate uncompressed color storage is 8 MiB for the leaf mip chain plus 1.33 MiB for the crown mip chain, with an additional depth attachment and driver overhead; these are estimates, not measured GPU memory. The 200-card crown is rendered once during initialization.
